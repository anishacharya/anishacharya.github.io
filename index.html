Anish Acharya is a Senior Research Scientist at AWS, where he works on multi-agent AI systems. His research asks what can be guaranteed when the signals a system learns from — its data, its supervision, its own evaluations — cannot be trusted, and how agents can improve themselves without drifting. Previously at Meta FAIR, Microsoft Research, and Alexa AI.

Research interests

  • Recursive self improvement and agentic systems
  • Robust estimation under contamination
  • Evaluation under adversarial and biased supervision
  • Non-convex, distributed, and combinatorial optimization
  • Data- and parameter-efficient learning
