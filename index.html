Anish Acharya, PhD

Senior Research Scientist
Amazon Web Services
Austin, Texas

{% include idlinks.html %}

Anish Acharya works on post-training and recursive self improvement: systems that revise their own prompts, tools, orchestration, policy, and weights. His research asks what can be guaranteed when the signals such a system learns from — its data, its supervision, its own evaluations — cannot be trusted, and how a self-improving loop can be made to compound rather than drift.

Research interests

  • Post-training and recursive self improvement
  • Policy optimization and RLHF
  • Continual learning
  • Personalized foundation models
  • Prompt and orchestration optimization
  • Robust reward modeling
  • LLM-as-judge, juries, and debate
  • Scalable oversight and agent evaluation
  • Multi-agent systems
  • Robust estimation and non-convex optimization
