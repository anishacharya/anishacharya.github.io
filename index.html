I am a Senior Research Scientist at AWS. I received my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin in 2025, where I was advised by Inderjit S. Dhillon and Sujay Sanghavi.

I am broadly interested in learning theory. My research studies learning as a feedback process under imperfect information: how systems construct objectives from incomplete feedback, update their models or policies under constraints, validate behavior, and use the results to guide the next round of learning.
